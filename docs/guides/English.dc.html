Staff guide

Update your details in the HR portal

A few simple steps from your phone or computer. Saved at once, then checked by HR.

About 10 minutes Phone or computer Your data stays private
1

Sign in to the portal

Open dev.hr.nama.omanste.com, choose your name from the list, then press:

Sign in
2

Open “My profile”

It is the last item in the menu. On a phone, tap “More” in the bottom bar, then “My profile”.

MoreMy profile
3

Upload the required documents

RequiredCivil IDPassportPersonal photo

In “Documents”, choose the type and upload the file. Your personal details (Arabic and English name, civil number, date of birth, nationality) come from your civil ID; the passport gives only its number and expiry. They are shown before and after. Untick anything that’s wrong, then “Update profile”. When one expires, press “Renew” and upload the new one.

Upload file🔄 Renew
4

Your photo

Take or upload a photo, or take it from your ID in “Documents”: move the square over your face, then save.

Take photoUse as profile photo
5

Contact and address

Edit
ContactMobile, personal number, personal e-mail — pick the country (flag, Oman first, search by name or code), then type the digits; an Omani number has 8 digits
AddressPermanent address (wilayat), current address (area in Muscat), way and building
Emergency contactName, relationship (a family member from the list; “Other family member”: write who), phone — all three together

Area not listed? Choose “Other…” at the end of the list and type it. Your work e-mail is added by HR.

6

Bank details

Choose your bank, then type your account number and IBAN as shown in your bank app:

OM00 0000 0000 0000 0000 000

Only you, HR and Accounts see them, for your salary transfer.

7

Qualifications

Press “Edit” on the Qualifications card: qualification, specialisation, experience, languages, certificates and skills, in Arabic and English.

EditSave
8

Your shift

Your shift is on the “Job” card. HR or the system admin sets it; a change applies from the next day. Your expected check-out time shows on Home.

Flexible · in 08:30–09:15, out 9 hours later10–5 · 10–6
9

Other documents

If any

Resume, academic certificate, course certificates, a copy of your contract, visa, medical notes and others: choose the type, then take a photo or upload it. Your documents are grouped by type, and we remind you a month before your ID, passport or visa expires.

Take photoUpload file
10

HR checks your updates

Your changes are saved at once and show this label until HR reviews them:

Awaiting HR verification

If HR puts an old value back, you get a notification with the reason.

11

Add your own events

In “Calendar”, pick the day, press “Add event” and choose who sees it:

Personal — only meShared with all staff

A shared event shows on everyone’s calendar with your name. You can delete it any time.

12

Ask for leave

On “Home” press “Apply for leave”, choose the type and dates, write the reason and send. For sick leave, attach the medical report. Your request has two approvals:

1 · Your direct manager approves→2 · HR gives the final approval→✓ Approved

You’re notified at each step, and the days come off your balance only after HR’s approval. You can cancel the request while it’s still waiting.

Umrah leave: up to 7 days once a year, taken from your annual leave balance (company policy). Attach the visa or booking to the request.

Maternity and paternity leave: maternity and child-care leave show for women, paternity leave for men. If yours doesn't show, ask HR to record your gender in your profile.

13

Check in and out

On “Home” press “Check in”. First your location (required), then Face ID or your fingerprint. If Face ID doesn’t work, take a photo instead — two more tries, after that the check-in is saved as “Unverified” for your manager to review. At the end of the day press “Check out”. Flexible shift: arrive between 08:30 and 09:15; you’re reminded if you haven’t checked in.

📍 At a work site: counts at once⚠️ Outside, Face ID failed or Unverified: your manager accepts or rejects

Away from the work sites, write the reason; the check-in is saved with ⚠️ and the distance. If your manager rejects it, the day counts as a missing punch — ask for a correction.

Camera black? Wait a few seconds, then press “📷 Open the phone’s camera” and take the photo. If the window doesn’t show, close the portal and open it again.

🔐 The first time: press “Register this phone” so the phone knows you by your face or fingerprint; your face or fingerprint is never stored in the portal. New phone? Register it at check-in or in “My profile” › “My phones”. A phone without it? Contact HR.

14

Personal break

Need to step out during the day? In “Permissions” ask for a “Personal break” with the time. Once your manager approves it, Home shows the button; press it when you leave and press “Back to work” when you return:

Ask for a personal break→Manager approves→Start the break→Back to work

The minutes you actually took are recorded. Leaving and coming back without an approved permission or break is flagged ⚠️ as time away.

15

Forgot to check out, or stayed late?

10 minutes after your shift ends (or the time the system admin sets) you’re asked: “Check out now” or “I’m still working” with a reason. Checking out more than 30 minutes after the shift also asks for the reason:

Reason→Manager approves or adjusts→HR

Forgot to check out? That day’s hours don’t count until a correction is approved, and the next day’s check-in is never added to it. Press “Ask for a correction” on Home the next morning.

16

My attendance

In “My attendance” each day is one line: check-in, expected check-out, check-out, hours, late, left early and after hours. Tap 📍 to see the place, 📷 for the photo, 🗺️ for the map; ⚠️ means the check-in was flagged. Choose the source at the top:

Online onlyFingerprint onlyCombined (📱 online · 👆 fingerprint)

A day missing a punch? Press “Ask for a correction” on that day. “Download my report (PDF)” opens a preview first: zoom, change the month or the source, then Download or Print.

17

Turn on notifications on your device

When you sign in, a window asks to “Turn on notifications on this device”: press “Turn on notifications”, then “Allow”, and your tasks, approvals and check-in reminders will appear on screen even with the portal closed. “Later” asks again in 3 days — or turn them on any time:

“More” or “Notifications”→Turn on notifications→Allow→Send a test ✓

iPhone: open the portal in Safari → Share → “Add to Home Screen”, open it from its icon, then turn notifications on (iOS 16.4 or later). Computer: Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

18

Are your details wrong?

In “Documents”, press “🔍 Read and update details” next to “Download” on your civil ID or passport: it reads the document again, shows only what differs from your profile, and saves what you tick. If something is still wrong, report it:

🔍 Read and update details⚠ Report a mistake💬 Feedback

“⚠ Report a mistake” in “Personal info”: choose the field, write what’s right or what’s wrong, and pick the document it should match. “💬 Feedback” on a document: a comment on how it was read. A report doesn’t change your profile; the system admin reviews it and fixes the reading.

Change your password On a phone: “More” → “Change password”; on a computer: the 🔑 button at the top. Forgot it? HR can reset it. Need help? Contact the HR department.
