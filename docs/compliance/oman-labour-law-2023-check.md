# HR Portal vs Oman Labour Law (Royal Decree 53/2023)

Date: 2026-10-26 · Scope: leave rules, working time and probation in the Nama HR portal (phase 1)

> **Status: for review by Nama's legal adviser.** This check was prepared from the official text of Royal Decree 53/2023 as quoted on decree.om (English) and qanoon.om / mol.gov.om (Arabic). The Arabic text is the authoritative one. Article numbers below are from the English consolidated text.
> The source sites could not be opened directly from the build environment, so each point should be confirmed against the Arabic text before the rules go live.
> Contracts that were in force before the law may keep more favourable terms.

Legend: ✅ the portal meets the law · ⚠️ partly / needs a setting · ❌ gap · 🔵 company policy (more generous or not covered by the law) — confirm in writing

## 1. Annual leave

| Law | Portal today | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Art. 78:** at least **30 days** a year with gross wage. | 30 days credited each leave year. | ✅ |
| **Art. 78:** leave can't be taken before **6 months** from the joining date. | Annual leave is blocked until join date + 6 months. | ✅ |
| **Art. 78:** the worker can't waive annual leave. | No waiver function exists. | ✅ |
| **Art. 78:** a worker who didn't use their leave may **keep a balance of up to 30 days**, *unless the non-use was due to the interest of work*. In that case the balance is kept in full. | The total is capped at 60 days (30 carried + 30 new). Any excess is **forfeited automatically** at the anniversary. Imported balances above 60 (106, 92, 74 days…) are cut at the next anniversary. | ❌ The automatic forfeit ignores the "interest of work" exception. Days lost because the company refused or postponed leave can't lawfully be forfeited. |
| **Art. 81:** leave may be **split**, and the employer may **postpone** it by up to **6 months**. | Splitting is allowed. Postponement isn't tracked. | ⚠️ |
| **Art. 81:** the worker must take **at least 30 days every 2 years**. | Not checked. | ❌ |
| **Art. 81:** untaken days may be paid in cash (basic wage), **only with the worker's written consent**. | Not supported. Payroll is a later phase. | ⚠️ |
| Commentary: the **unused balance is paid at end of service** (gross wage). | The ledger keeps the balance after resignation, so payroll can use it later. | ✅ — but only if nothing was wrongly forfeited (see the carry-over row) |
| Whether days are counted as **calendar or working days**, and whether **official holidays** inside the leave count | Calendar days, weekends included (company decision). Official holidays inside the leave are counted. | 🔵 Not settled by the text found. Confirm with the legal adviser. Many employers exclude official holidays. |

## 2. Early leave (hours deducted from annual leave)

| Law | Portal | Status |
|---|---|---|
| The law has **no hourly-permission leave**. Annual leave is defined in days. | Early-leave hours are deducted from annual leave (8 h = 1 day). | 🔵 Company policy. Deducting from a statutory leave balance should rest on a written policy that employees accept, or be changed. |

## 3. Sick leave

| Law | Portal | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Art. 82:** up to **182 days a year**, paid on a scale of the gross wage: days 1–21 at 100%, 22–35 at 75%, 36–70 at 50%, 71–182 at 35%. | Recorded with approval. No yearly total, no limit, no pay band. | ❌ |
| **Art. 47:** the illness must be proven by a **medical certificate**. | The medical note is optional. | ❌ |
| The Social Protection Law (RD 52/2023) also covers sick pay. The employer pays the first days and the fund pays the rest. | — | ⚠️ For payroll later. |

## 4. Special leave with full wage (Art. 84 — proof is required for each)

| Leave | Law | Portal |
|---|---|---|
| Marriage | 3 days, once during service | ❌ missing |
| Paternity | 7 days; the child born alive; taken before the child is 98 days old | ❌ missing |
| Maternity | 98 days in total, at most 14 of them before the birth (medical recommendation) | ❌ missing |
| Bereavement | 10 days for a wife, son or daughter. 3 days for a father, mother, grandparent, brother or sister. 2 days for an uncle or aunt (paternal or maternal). A Muslim widow gets 130 days, a non-Muslim widow 14 days. The list is exhaustive. | ⚠️ A generic "bereavement" type exists, with no relation or day limit. |
| Hajj | 15 days, once during service, after 1 continuous year | ❌ missing |
| Exams | Up to 15 days a year, **Omani** workers studying | ❌ missing |
| Accompanying a patient | 15 days a year, **Omani** workers, spouse or relative up to the 2nd degree | ❌ missing |
| Child care (Art. 83) | Unpaid, up to 1 year, for a working mother; she pays the social-protection contributions | ❌ missing |
| Special unpaid leave (Art. 80) | At the employer's discretion; the worker pays the contributions; it counts as service but not towards the end-of-service gratuity | ❌ missing |
| Emergency leave | **Not in the 2023 law** (the old law had 6 days) | 🔵 The portal records it without deduction. That's a company benefit, and it's fine to keep. |

## 5. Working time, rest days, holidays, probation

| Law | Portal | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Art. 70:** at most **8 actual hours a day, 40 a week**. The rest/meal break isn't counted. Ramadan: **6 h a day / 30 h a week** for Muslim workers. | The day is 09:00–17:00, and 8 h = 1 day. There is no Ramadan schedule, so early leave is always measured to 17:00. | ⚠️ A Ramadan hours setting is needed. Also confirm that 09:00–17:00 with a break stays within 8 actual hours. |
| Weekly rest: at least **2 consecutive paid days**. | Fri/Sat assumed. Not configurable. | ✅ |
| **Art. 71:** work on a rest day or official holiday is paid **100% of the daily basic wage on top of the day's wage**, or given as **compensatory leave**. The sources differ on the premium rate. | A "worked on a holiday" request adds +1 day to the annual balance. | ⚠️ Compensatory leave is allowed. These days shouldn't be lost to the 60-day cap, and the rate should be confirmed. |
| **Art. 37:** probation is at most **3 months** for monthly-paid staff (2 months otherwise), only once per employer, and counts as service. Either side may end it with 7 days' notice. | Probation is fixed at 3 months. | ✅ for monthly-paid staff |

## 6. Recommended changes

**A — needed to meet the law**
1. **Stop forfeiting automatically.** When a balance goes over the cap at an anniversary, flag it to HR instead. HR then chooses either "forfeit" or "keep — not used because of work needs". Both outcomes are recorded with the reason. The imported balances above 60 days need this decision first.
2. **Add the statutory leave types from Art. 80, 83 and 84** with the legal limits: once only, days per year, length of service, Omani only, the relation for bereavement, and women only for maternity and child care. Each of these requires proof to be attached. A **gender** field is needed on the profile.
3. **Sick leave:**
   - a running yearly total, with a stop at 182 days
   - the pay band shown to HR and Accounts (100 / 75 / 50 / 35%)
   - a medical certificate required before approval

**B — should do**
4. A **Ramadan hours** setting: dates and end of the working day. Early leave would be measured to that time.
5. Keep "worked on a holiday" days out of the 60-day cap.
6. Warn HR when someone hasn't taken 30 days in the last 2 years (Art. 81).
7. Record **postponements** (at most 6 months) and **cash payment for leave** (basic wage, with a written consent document). The cash payment would be completed by the payroll phase.

**C — company policy, to confirm in writing**
8. Early leave deducted from annual leave.
9. Counting calendar days, and official holidays inside annual leave.
10. Keeping emergency leave as a company benefit.

## 7. Implemented (2026-10-26, branch `dev`)

All items in A, B and C from section 6 are now in the portal:

1. **No automatic forfeit.** A balance over 60 days at an anniversary opens a **balance review** for HR (*Balance reviews* page). HR records either "keep — work needs" or "forfeit", with a reason. If HR forfeits, the amount is at most what is over the cap on the day of the decision. Days earned by working on a holiday are not counted towards the cap. Imported balances (106, 92, 74 days…) are kept until HR decides.
2. **Statutory leaves**, each with its legal limits; the server refuses anything over them:
   - marriage, paternity, maternity
   - bereavement, where the days follow the relation and the widow's leave is for women
   - Hajj, exam leave, accompanying a patient
   - child care (unpaid) and special unpaid leave

   The employee profile now has a **gender** field (maternity, paternity, child care). Its **nationality** decides eligibility for exam leave and patient accompaniment (Omani staff only).
3. **Proof before approval** for sick leave (medical certificate) and for the Art. 84 leaves. The approvals list shows "no document".
4. **Sick leave:** at most 182 days a calendar year. The request page shows the pay split (100 / 75 / 50 / 35%).
5. **Ramadan hours** in Settings: the dates and the end of the working day. Early leave is measured to that time.
6. **Early leave** is recorded in hours only. It is **no longer deducted** from annual leave, and the 6-month rule no longer applies to it.
7. **The 2-year rule:** HR's home page lists anyone who hasn't taken 30 days of annual leave in the last 2 years.
8. **Postponement:** the approver can postpone a pending annual leave by at most 6 months. This approves it on the new dates and records the change.
9. **Cash for unused leave:** HR can pay leave days in cash. This needs the employee's signed consent uploaded as a "consent" document first.

Still open, for the legal adviser:
- whether official holidays inside annual leave count as leave days
- the holiday-work pay rate
- the wage figures for cash payments and sick pay, which will be handled in the payroll phase
